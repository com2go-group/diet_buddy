Safety & privacy

Healthy habits, not extremes

  • 🛡️ Safe by design

    Targets never go below 1,200 kcal (women) or 1,500 kcal (men) a day, or your resting energy needs, and weekly loss is capped at 1% of your body weight.

  • 🔒 Your data stays yours

    Stored in the EU and visible only to you. Never sold, never used for advertising. Export or delete everything at any time.

  • 🤝 Support when it matters

    The coach won’t encourage extreme restriction and points you to professional help if you’re struggling.

  • ✅ You’re in control

    Optional features like AI body scans and wellness insights only run with your separate permission, and you can switch them off anytime.

DietBuddy gives general nutrition and fitness information and is not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new diet, especially if you have a medical condition or are pregnant. DietBuddy is for adults aged 18 and over.

FAQ

Questions and answers

Is DietBuddy free?

Yes. Your plan, food logging, water, check-ins, progress and a daily allowance of coach messages are free, with ads. Premium removes ads and adds unlimited coaching, full meal plans and more.

Where do the calorie and macro numbers come from?

From USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts. The AI helps recognise foods and plan meals, but the numbers always come from a food database.

Does it work with my smart scale or watch?

Yes, through Apple Health on iPhone or Health Connect on Android. Most scales and wearables, including Withings, Garmin, Fitbit, Apple Watch, Oura and Samsung Health, share their data there.

Is my health data safe?

Your data is stored in the EU, is visible only to you and is never sold or used for ads. AI features receive only what each feature needs. You can export or delete everything in Profile → Privacy & Data.

How do I cancel Premium?

Premium is managed by the App Store or Google Play. Cancel anytime in your store account settings, at least 24 hours before the next renewal.

Is DietBuddy medical advice?

No. It gives general guidance and estimates. Please talk to a doctor or dietitian about medical conditions, pregnancy or eating concerns.

Who can use DietBuddy?

Adults aged 18 and over. The app is currently available in English.
