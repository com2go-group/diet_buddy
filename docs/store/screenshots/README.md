## Before submitting

- **Status bar:** web captures have no phone status bar. For the final set, capture on an iOS
  simulator and Android emulator with `.maestro/screenshots/store-screenshots.yaml` (it signs in
  with a demo account and takes the same six screenshots), or add the images to device frames.
- **Real content:** the coach conversation was written for the demo account. For the final set,
  hold the conversation with the real coach on the demo account so the screenshots show actual
  replies (App Store Review Guideline 2.3.3 expects screenshots to show the app in use).
- **Captions and order:** the stores allow up to 10 images; add short captions if you like
  (for example "Know your numbers every day", "An AI coach that fits your plan"). Don't show
  prices; they differ by country.
- **Tablets:** DietBuddy doesn't target iPad or Android tablets, so no tablet screenshots are
  needed unless you enable tablet support.
